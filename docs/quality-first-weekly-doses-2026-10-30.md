# Quality-first weekly doses

The planner could leave quality objectives unfinished even with available days:
combinations halved each method's role budget, a later balancing pass could split
that dose again, and future reservations protected only minimum exposures.

Version 68 assigns each combination part its own remaining component objective
and role/Recovery budget. Both parts retain complete repetitions and their own
minimum effective structures. Warm-up and cooldown are shared once. Combinations
still require a demonstrated shortage of separate session opportunities.

Future quality reservations now protect an executable objective dose and its
preparation in the current microcycle, with a minimum-dose alternative. A causal
replay validates the complete reservation after earlier work. Later microcycles
initially reserve minimum exposures, then reconcile with the methods actually
selected. Easy allocation subtracts already reserved preparation/rest work so it
does not fill that same component twice.

Whole repetitions prefer the next higher value when admissible. Building work in
Z3–Z5 may round up by at most 10% of its role dose after Recovery; weekly component
coverage still cannot exceed 110%. Easy and maintenance role ceilings retain
their existing limits. Measured automatic session exposure allows the same 10%
tolerance for building Z3–Z5 structures; explicit daily/weekly time limits remain
hard limits. These are coach policies, not new physiological thresholds.

Mandatory Z1 preparation for unfinished Z3–Z5/strength objectives can use a
separate frozen reserve: 10% of eligible direct-Q targets plus their existing
easy-reference budget. Easy work cannot consume this extra reserve. The extra
load enters Q, effective load, 7/40 and Recovery normally; it is identified as
support rather than reported as core objective coverage. Actual and planned
expenditure consume the same reserve, including after regeneration. Manual,
unknown-history, re-entry and taper restrictions retain their separate gates.

Validation covers independent combination doses, one Recovery adjustment,
nonuniform source repetitions, per-part minima, reduced Recovery after locking,
hard dose/time/component bounds, reserve expenditure and complete causal plans.
No athlete profile, approved plan, method source standard or historical activity
is edited by this change.

The search remains bounded. A small residual can remain when the next complete
repetition crosses a component, dose, time or preparation bound. Such a result
must retain its actual coverage and `hard_limit_proven: false`; exhausting tested
variants does not establish that every possible schedule is infeasible.
