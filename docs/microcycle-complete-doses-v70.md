# Microcycle frequency and complete reduced doses

A previous microcycle's ordinary sessions could exhaust the next cycle's
frequency quota, while its Q objectives had already restarted. Native methods
also required 25% of continuous Tmax (5–10% for some variants), even when a
smaller complete recipe fitted the remaining budget. Together these gates
could turn available training days into rest.

Version 70 counts ordinary actual, planned and reserved sessions in the same
anchored microcycle as the objectives. Grouped recording identity and daily
slots remain unchanged. Key-session exposure still uses rolling seven days and
existing spacing; strength, Q/E, Recovery and explicit time limits retain
their existing checks across cycle boundaries.

Native dose selection now starts at the recipe's declared work minimum and
uses complete repetitions. Short introductory and supporting variants retain
their own repetition counts; combinations must complete each constituent
recipe. Published library standards and strength structures keep their own
minima. No generic percentage of aerobic Tmax imposes an additional floor.
Ordinary capacity-dosed native intervals retain their existing permission to
use a single whole effort; an example repeat count is not a source protocol.
The desired role dose and its upper limits remain separate from this minimum.
Reduced work keeps its actual purpose, Q/E and reported coverage; preparation
and prescribed pauses remain included.

The audit also covers these related cases:

- Future reservations reduce a desired dose to an intermediate complete
  variant when time or observed session exposure prevents the full dose.
- Preparation reservations try a complete smaller core if their desired dose
  does not fit. First exposures take priority over extra work in components
  already trained, so optional easy work cannot erase another quality.
- Native repetition minima use their exact unit, so three 45-second efforts
  remain 2.25 minutes instead of an incompatible half-minute floor.
- An upper whole variant that worsens the residual Q gap cannot discard a
  useful smaller variant. Beneficial admissible upper rounding still applies.
- New locked doses retain their recorded contract. Legacy locks lacking dose
  evidence retain their existing review requirement.

Regression checks exercise a 31-Q ceiling with continuous and threshold
interval recipes, rejection below a complete structure, unchanged readiness
when only frequency changes, exact repetition units, per-part combination
minima, locked-dose replay and useful lower source variants. End-to-end
generation still reports actual shortfalls; bounded search is not proof that
all other schedules are infeasible. Athlete targets and historical records
are not modified.
